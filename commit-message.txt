Initial scaffold: agentic SDLC orchestrator design and skeleton

Design and structural scaffold for a graph-based SDLC orchestration
engine, plus the URL shortener it will build as its test subject.

Orchestrator (the deliverable):
- docs/orchestration-graph.yaml: 7-node SDLC DAG as data, with
  entry/exit gates, retry policy, rollback actions and re-plan
  triggers declared per node. Verified acyclic.
- orchestrator/: graph_model, executor, gates, decision_log, metrics
  as structural skeletons. Dataclasses, enums, signatures and control
  flow are final; bodies are TODO(impl).
- Parallel wave (testing || documentation) and the release-readiness
  join barrier are derived from the edge set, not declared.

Governance:
- Human gates persist-and-exit at node boundaries, so a pause survives
  process death and is inspectable on disk (DEC-0007).
- Five structural high-impact criteria gate approvals (DEC-0006);
  greenfield yields 1 gate, brownfield 2.
- Approvals bind to artifact content hashes, so re-planning invalidates
  approvals for changed artifacts rather than inheriting them.
- Lineage is append-only by construction.

Docs:
- research-notes.md: sourced survey of 8 workflow engines, graph
  theory, HITL patterns, SRE metrics and URL shortener fundamentals,
  each with what we borrow and what we reject.
- decision-log.md: DEC-0001..DEC-0011 with alternatives and rationale.
- example-run.md: worked trace including failure ladder and
  re-planning, doubling as an 18-assertion test spec.
- Three scenarios with genuinely different graph traversals.

Status: specified and scaffolded; not yet runnable end to end.
orchestrator/README.md tracks per-file status and build order.
